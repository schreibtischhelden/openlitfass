Third-party notices for the openLitfass web front end
=====================================================

This file is published with every openLitfass site.

Tabler Icons
------------

The category and filter icons, the attribute and navigation icons in
js/app.js and js/map.js, and the icons in index.html and the page templates
use path data from Tabler Icons, https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons.
The calendar icons of the date bar are derived from its "calendar" icon.

markdown-pages.js and the libraries it loads
--------------------------------------------

The Markdown pages of an instance (seite.html?page=<name>) are rendered in
the browser by markdown-pages.js v2.0.0,
https://github.com/schreibtischhelden/markdown-pages.js (a fork of
https://github.com/dandalpiaz/markdown-pages.js), with these libraries:

- Showdown 2.1.0, https://github.com/showdownjs/showdown (MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2018,2021 ShowdownJS)
- DOMPurify 3.5.0, https://github.com/cure53/DOMPurify (Apache License 2.0
  or Mozilla Public License 2.0, (c) Cure53 and other contributors; used
  under the Apache License 2.0)
- showdown-forms 1.0.0, https://github.com/schreibtischhelden/showdown-forms
  (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Tobias Fichtner)

They are copied unchanged into vendor/markdown-pages/; the full licence
texts are next to each file (vendor/markdown-pages/LICENSE and
vendor/markdown-pages/assets/vendor/*/LICENSE).
